Research

Radial velocities of WASP-18 from archival spectra

Aug – Sep 2026Guided by Jebraan Mudholkar, master’s student, LMU Munich

An independent extraction of stellar radial velocities from publicly available WASP-18 spectra, using line-by-line Gaussian fitting, to recover the periodic signal induced by its known hot Jupiter.

The pipeline takes a star’s name, queries the ESO archive through astroquery, then plots and normalises each spectrum. It fits Gaussians to absorption lines at given rest wavelengths to measure their observed wavelengths, and converts the shifts into a radial velocity per spectrum.

Python, SciPy, Astropy, astroquery · RVsFromSpectra on GitHub

Times of minima of overcontact eclipsing binaries

Feb – Mar 2026Guided by Ravi Raja Pothuneni, PhD student, Osmania University

A pipeline for period-variation analysis of two overcontact eclipsing binaries, V2284 Cygni and V1918 Cygni.

Given an eclipsing binary’s name, it searches MAST for available light curves, processes and plots them, fits Gaussian profiles to each eclipse, and calculates the times of minima from the fitted profiles.

Python, SciPy, Astropy, Matplotlib · TimesOfMinimaPipeline on GitHub
